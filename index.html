ALPINE SKIING / PRESEASON

Six weeks to stronger skiing

Strength · braking control · balance · endurance

Your training week

Replace part of your usual leg training. Leave at least 48 hours between demanding leg sessions; skip Saturday if unusually sore.

Progression rules

RPE 6 ≈ 4 reps in reserve; RPE 8 ≈ 2. Never train to failure. Increase weight 2.5–5% or reps, not both, if recovered. Add 5–10 seconds to holds when stable.

Finish the last challenging leg session 4–5 days before skiing. Keep the last 2–3 days easy.

Shorter trip lead time: four weeks → weeks 1, 2, 4, 6; two weeks → week 1 and a light week 6. Eight weeks → repeat weeks 2 and 4.

Workout library

Warm up 5–8 min with easy cycling or walking, then 8 squats, 8 hip hinges and easy first-exercise reps.

Exercise guide

Readiness log

Track comfortable performance, not maximum effort. This is not medical clearance.

On the mountain

Warm up 5–10 minutes, start with easy runs, take frequent breaks and stop before fatigue compromises turns. Hydrate, eat and sleep regularly; start conservatively at altitude.

Stop for sharp joint pain, swelling, locking or instability. Seek clinical advice if symptoms persist or if you have an injury or medical restriction.
